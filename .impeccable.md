## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to greykit.com's games page, and the author's friends who know PumaCity from the author's old game. They play a short run (30, 60 or 90 days) at a desk, in a spare quarter of an hour, and come back to beat their own high score. Anyone who has played *Dope Wars* should be trading within a minute. A newcomer should learn the loop from the screen itself (buy here, carry it there, sell, pay off The Boss), not from the help.

### Brand Personality
**Sunny, owned, a little crooked.** greykit.com's voice is wry, specific and brief; never zany, never emoji. The joke is the setting.

PumaCity is a **tourist enclave**: late-90s Pleasanton, California. It has outdoor malls, office parks, cul-de-sacs, and a starport. Everything in it is owned by **Casey Benton** (she/her). She is the owner, the landlord, the loan shark and, effectively, the dictator. The trade runs underneath a pleasant, well-kept surface.

**Emotional goal: a sunny place with a back room.** Bright, orderly districts on the surface. The terminal is your back channel into the trade: prices, moves, who's after you. Tension spikes at encounters; satisfaction comes when a carried load sells high.

### The districts (canon from the author)
- **Market Plaza (Downtown)**: a Californian open-air outdoor mall. Tourists with money. The outfitter (pouches, weapons, accessories) is here.
- **PVN Tower**: an office tower. The theater is at ground level, the nightclub above it, and Casey's office in the penthouse; she's the loan shark.
- **Starport**: where the ship lands (not capitalised, not a proper name). It trades goods with other ports.
- **The Convention Center**: near the starport. It hosts various events.
- **The Ranch**: an old horse ranch turned into grow fields.
- **The Vault**: a Fallout-style underground vault, fully themed, standing ready for PumaCity's leadership in case of war.
- **Suburbs**: single-family homes, all rented out by Casey Benton. The vet is here.
- The police are **the Watchers**, led by **Watch-Captain Joyeuse**. Never expand "PVN".

### Aesthetic Direction
- **The Tactical tribe**, shared with PumaRally, PumaTower and PumaTycoon:
  - deep navy ground `#05070f`
  - cyan `#54f2ff` for the live thing
  - amber `#ffb000` for warnings and news
  - green for gains, red for losses and danger
  - monospace throughout, as the terminal's voice
  - dark only
- **The playfield is a terminal set inside the city.** It is centered in the window. Behind it, original line-drawn scenery shows the **current district**:
  - the mall's palms and awnings
  - the Tower's theater marquee, the club above it, and the penthouse
  - the ship on its pad
  - the ranch fences and grow rows
  - the cog-shaped blast door
  - the cul-de-sac rooftops
  - the convention hall's banners

  The scenery sits behind the terminal and is subordinate to it: it is low contrast, it never competes with prices, and the terminal stays legible over it.
- **Late-90s, not cyberpunk.** The reference is suburban California at dusk: stucco, palms, parking-lot lights, office-park glass. It is not rain-soaked neon noir, and it is not synthwave grids.
- **Anti-references:** glassmorphism, glowing-neon-on-black, purple gradients, generic "hacker" green-on-black.
- **No build, no dependencies, no web fonts, offline at play time.** Art is inline SVG, drawn for this game.
- **Desktop first.** Judge at 1280–1600px. The stacked phone layout must stay usable and unbroken, and the scenery may simplify or drop away there.

### Design Principles
1. **The market is the hero.** Prices and what you hold are the first thing the eye reads; the scenery and lore frame them and never crowd them.
2. **Arriving is an event.** Travel is the game's heartbeat. A new district should look and feel like somewhere new (scenery, name, headline) before you read a single price.
3. **Show the consequence where the decision is made.** Costs, room, heat and risk appear beside the button that spends them.
4. **Casey owns everything.** Her presence (landlord, lender, Boss) should be felt across districts, not confined to one office.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA contrast and full keyboard play. Reduced motion is respected. Price movement never relies on red/green alone.
