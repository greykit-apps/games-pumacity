# PumaCity

**Buy low, sell high, and keep one step ahead of the Watchers.** A puma-flavoured trading game in the spirit of *Dope Wars* (John E. Dell, 1984), set on the streets of PumaCity. You start with $1,500 and owe Casey Benton, The Boss, $8,000 at 10% a day.

**Play:** [greykit.com/games/pumacity](https://greykit.com/games/pumacity/)

![Day 12 of a Wanted run at the Starport: the terminal over the ship on its pad, the Debt box warning of the goons, and a headline that has crashed Skooma to a bargain](screenshots/market.png)

## How it plays

Pick a difficulty (Tourist, Regular, Wanted or Custom), a length of 30, 60 or 90 days, and a name. Every trip between PumaCity's seven districts takes a day, and prices roll fresh at each stop; the *vs usual* column says whether a price is a bargain or sky-high. Selling and fighting raise your heat, and heat and a heavy load draw the Watchers. The debt compounds daily: go too long without a real payment and The Boss sends her goons. Encounters roll on arrival (Watchers, rival dealers, Casey herself, goons, strays, muggers), and every choice to run, fight or give up shows its cost first.

On the last day you cash out: score is cash plus bank minus debt, and anything still in your pouch is worth nothing. Each difficulty keeps its own top ten.

## Keys

| Key | |
|---|---|
| `1`–`9`, `↑` `↓` | select a good |
| `B` / `S` | buy / sell it |
| `T`, then `1`–`7` | travel |
| `R` `F` `U`, `Y` / `N` | encounters: run, fight, give up; accept or decline |
| `M` | sound |
| `?` | how to play, with every key |

Everything is also a button.

## Saves

Kept in the browser's `localStorage` under keys starting `pumacity_`. **Settings → Forget everything** clears them.
